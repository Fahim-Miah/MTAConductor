### Example 2: Headway Calculation
**Question**: A train runs every 6 minutes during peak hours. How many trains run in 1 hour?
- A) 8
- B) 10 ✓
- C) 12
- D) 15

**Explanation**: 60 minutes ÷ 6 minutes = 10 trains
