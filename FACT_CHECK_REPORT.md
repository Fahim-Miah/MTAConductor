### Question 452 - INCORRECT ANSWER
**Current:**
- Q: "The MTA's customer service phone number is:"
- Choices: ["311", "511", "711", "911"]
- Correct: 0 (311)
- Explanation: "311 is the MTA's customer service phone number."

**Issue:** This is INCORRECT. The MTA's customer service number is 511, not 311.
- 311 is NYC's general non-emergency services line
- 511 is the MTA's transit information line
- 711 is for TTY/relay services
- 911 is for emergencies

**Fix Required:** Change correct answer to 1 (511) and update explanation.
